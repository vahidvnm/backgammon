# Third-Party Notices

## Wooden dice recording

`app/src/main/res/raw/dice_roll.wav` is adapted from **“Wooden dice on wodden table roll”** by Wuzzy, sourced from OpenGameArt.org and released under **CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**.

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/wooden-dice-on-wodden-table-roll

The game mix layers two of the original wooden-die-on-wood recordings with a short timing offset, applies light cleanup and normalization, and adds a short silent tail to align with the visual settle animation.
