# AI Backgammon

## Short description
Premium offline backgammon with strategic AI, carved boards, coaching and clocks.

## Full description
Play classic backgammon on a richly carved board built for tactile, focused play.

AI Backgammon is completely offline: no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet connection required. Challenge distinct on-device opponents, play locally with a friend, or enable optional coaching while learning legal routes and compound moves.

KEY FEATURES

• Complete standard backgammon movement rules
• Four AI personalities: Builder, Runner, Hunter and Master
• Five difficulty levels
• Standard doubling cube with Take, Drop and Redouble
• Direct compound checker moves using two dice or doubles
• Optional Coach guidance and simple play mode
• Rapid and Standard match clocks
• Safe unlimited Undo during the current session
• Automatic match restoration after leaving the app
• Multiple crafted boards, checker styles, dice and table scenes
• Natural wooden dice audio and optional haptic feedback
• Local two-player pass-and-play
• Fully offline, without ads or analytics

Every match is rendered with polished checkers, physical dice, carved storage wells and responsive layouts for landscape phones and tablets.

Classic strategy. Intelligent play.
