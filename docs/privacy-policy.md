# AI Backgammon Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 24, 2026

AI Backgammon is an offline board game. We designed it to work without an account, advertising identifier, analytics SDK, or network connection.

## Data collection

AI Backgammon does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, tracking, account creation, or in-app purchases.

## Data stored on the device

The app stores the following information locally on the player's device:

- game settings and appearance choices;
- the current match position, score, dice, doubling-cube state, and turn;
- local undo history while the app is running.

This information is used only to restore the player's game and preferences. It is not sent to the developer or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data according to Android behavior.

## Permissions

AI Backgammon may use the device vibration feature for optional haptic feedback. The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or notification permissions.

## Children

Because no personal information is collected, the app does not knowingly collect information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise device and game use as appropriate.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is published.
